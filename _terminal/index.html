the thesis · aeo / geo

AI speed is not the issue. AI direction is.

Your buyers stopped Googling. They ask an AI, read one answer, and act on it. If the AI can't read your site, can't understand what you do, and can't quote you — you're not in that answer. Doesn't matter how good the site looks. This is Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) / Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), and it's the whole game now.

01 Machine-legible HTML

Real content in the raw HTML — not walled behind JavaScript an AI fetcher can't run. If a bot can't read it, it can't rank it.

02 JSON-LD schema

Organization, Person, FAQPage, ItemList — the structured data that tells the model exactly who you are and what you do.

03 llms.txt + robots

An llms.txt at the root and robots.txt Allow: / — an open door for the AI crawlers, not a locked one.

04 Answer-first copy

Content written the way AI actually pulls answers: extractable, direct, structured to be quoted — so the model picks you.
