How AI decides who to name

AI speed is not the issue. AI direction is.

When someone asks a model for the best firms in your space, it doesn't guess. It reads. Three things decide whether it reads — and repeats — you.

Lever 01

Machine-legible structure

Real static HTML, JSON-LD schema, an llms.txt, robots that say yes. If an AI fetcher can't cleanly read you, you don't exist to it — no matter how the site looks to a human.
